4 The 3-Step Daily Executive Routine

How to get maximum clarity and output in under 15 minutes a day.

☀️ 2. Midday Execution (1:00 PM — 2 Mins)

📅 4. Sunday Strategic Reset (15 Mins)

• Open Weekly Review to view your velocity score.
• Review monthly OKRs in Monthly Goals.
• Outline the 3 big deliverables that will make next week a major success.

6 Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use this on my iPhone or Android device?

Yes. Open the link in Safari or Chrome, tap the Share/Menu icon, and select "Add to Home Screen". It will operate as a fast, standalone full-screen executive app.

Q: What does the "Overdue Action Notification" banner mean?

If a scheduled focus item (such as an important client proposal review) passes its scheduled hour without being checked off, the system alerts you so you can either complete it or reschedule it.

Q: Is my executive data secure?

Yes. All profile records, credentials, tasks, and notes are secured in an enterprise-grade Supabase PostgreSQL cloud database with Row Level Security (RLS) policies.

7 Smart Alarms, Persistent Follow-ups & Next-Day Rescheduling

The Managing Director's proactive execution partner. Instead of merely displaying tasks, WeGrow AI actively ensures scheduled routine work gets completed through configurable alarms and intelligent next-day carry-forward.

1. Advance Alarms & Chimes

Configurable 15-minute and 5-minute advance chime alerts, followed by an immediate task-start chime. For client meetings and branch visits, 30-minute preparation alerts ensure executive readiness.

2. Hourly Persistent Follow-ups

If a scheduled routine task remains incomplete after its start time, recurring reminders prompt you every 60 minutes during permitted working hours until checked off, snoozed, or rescheduled.

3. Automatic End-of-Day Pending

At close of business, unfinished tasks are automatically moved to the Pending Tasks backlog with original scheduled dates, notes, and priority preserved. Zero tasks are ever lost or blindly discarded.

4. Next-Day Slot Suggestions

Draft rescheduling proposals find free time slots for tomorrow without altering weekly templates or overwriting deep work blocks. Client meetings are protected and require explicit MD confirmation.
